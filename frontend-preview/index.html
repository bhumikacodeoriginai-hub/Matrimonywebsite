WHY CHOOSE US

Safety & Privacy First

Your security is our top priority. Advanced protection features built into every profile.

Photo Privacy Controls

Your photos are blurred for non-members. Grant access only to people you trust via Request Access.

Watermarked Photos

All photos are automatically watermarked with "Advaita Matrimony" to prevent misuse.

Contact Masking

Phone numbers and email IDs are hidden from free users. Only premium members can view contacts.

Manual Verification

Every profile is manually reviewed and approved by our admin team before going live.

UDID Verification

Disability certificate / UDID verification for authenticity and trust within the differently-abled community.

Screenshot Protection

Screenshot restrictions on mobile app to prevent unauthorized saving of profile photos.
